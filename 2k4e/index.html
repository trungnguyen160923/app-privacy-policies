The Application is a 2048-style puzzle game. It supports gameplay modes such as 4x4, 5x5, 6x6, and may show other board sizes in the menu. Some modes, such as 8x8 when available, may be playable without leaderboard score submission.

The Application uses the internet to provide account features, email OTP verification, device transfer, account recovery, leaderboard, score synchronization, rewarded advertisements, consent management, theme or resource updates, and security checks.

The Application may generate and store a device UUID. It may also create a device fingerprint from data such as device brand, device name, model, manufacturer, Android version, Android ID, and app version. The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide gameplay features.
